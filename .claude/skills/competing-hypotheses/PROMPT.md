# Pasteable ACH prompt

Copy everything below the line into any assistant, with your claim in the
bracket. Works on an AI answer, a number headed for a deck, health advice, or a
contract summary you did not read.

---

You are an intelligence analyst using the CIA's Analysis of Competing Hypotheses.

The claim I'm about to act on: [paste the claim or AI answer]

First list at least three other explanations that could also be true. One of them
must be "the source is simply wrong."

Then give me:

1. The claim restated with the confident wording removed
2. Each piece of evidence, and which explanations it fits or contradicts
3. The explanation with the least evidence against it
4. One check I can do myself that would settle it
5. A verdict: act, wait, or verify first

Rules: try to break the claim instead of defending it, mark anything you can't
source as unverified, and if a piece of evidence fits every explanation, tell me
it proves nothing.

---

## Why the last rule is the one that matters

Most of what feels like proof fits every explanation equally well. A detailed,
confident, internally consistent answer is exactly what you get from a correct
source *and* from a fabricating one — so detail, confidence and consistency are
not evidence for either. Once that row is struck from the table, what is usually
left is thinner than it felt.

## Getting more out of it

- **Name the hypotheses before looking at the evidence.** Listing them afterwards
  lets the evidence you already have decide which explanations get considered.
- **Push back on a skipped hypothesis.** If the assistant quietly drops "the
  source is simply wrong," ask it to rank that one explicitly.
- **Ask which single piece of evidence, if false, would flip the answer.** That
  is the thing to check, and it is often the one nobody sourced.
- **Take the verdict literally.** "Verify first" means run the check in (4)
  before acting, not read the analysis and feel reassured.
